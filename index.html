The story

Flamingos aren't built for the cold

Flora the flamingo has never seen Christmas. So when her flock flies off to the sunshine, she stays behind on her little snowy beach.

There's just one problem. Brrrr! Her long skinny legs are turning blue, and Flora has never owned a single sock. Luckily, she has three very good friends.

Friendship and kindness

A robin, a seal and a crab with a plan

Flora's friends huddle up and work through the night to make her something special. Then, on Christmas Day, Flora finds a way to give something back to the whole town.

  • A gentle story about friendship, kindness and finding where you belong
  • A funny twist at the end that makes everyone smile
  • Set by the seaside, with twinkling lights along the prom

Yes. The free Kindle app works on phones and tablets. A tablet shows the pictures best.

The author

Rosa Featherstone

Rosa Featherstone lives by the Yorkshire coast, where sea breezes, winter walks and a love of flamingos inspire her stories. She writes about kindness, belonging and the little things that make life magical.

Pink feathers. Shoreline pebbles. A little storytelling magic.
